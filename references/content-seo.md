# Content & E-E-A-T

Relevant, trustworthy, well-structured content — for both humans and AI.

## On-page essentials

- **Title tag:** unique per page, 50–60 chars, primary keyword near the front, brand at the end.
- **Meta description:** 150–160 chars, unique, value + soft CTA (it's a snippet, not a ranking factor
  directly, but it drives CTR).
- **H1:** exactly one, matching the page's intent; **H2/H3** for structure; no skipped levels.
- **Keyword & intent:** one primary intent per page; cover related questions; avoid cannibalising the same
  keyword across pages.

## Depth & originality

- Answer the question **completely**: definitions, examples, edge cases, and specifics.
- Be **original** — AI citations favour clear, verifiable, first-hand content.
- Date and source your claims; show an **updated** date for fresh content.

## E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust)

- **Author bios** with credentials; an **About** and **Contact** page; clear ownership.
- Cite **sources**; show real reviews/testimonials (honestly).
- Especially for YMYL (health/finance/legal): accuracy and transparency matter most.

## Structure that helps crawlers and AI

- Lead with the **direct answer**, then the detail (good for snippets and AI quotes).
- Use **lists, tables, and short paragraphs**.
- **FAQ** sections with matching `FAQPage` JSON-LD.
- **Breadcrumbs** + `BreadcrumbList`.
- **Descriptive internal links** (not "click here").

## Avoid

- Thin, duplicated, or auto-spun content; keyword stuffing; hidden text; misleading titles.
- Pages that exist only to capture a keyword with no value.

## Checklist

- [ ] unique titles + meta descriptions
- [ ] one H1, logical headings
- [ ] intent mapped, no cannibalisation
- [ ] complete, original answers
- [ ] E-E-A-T signals (author, about, contact, sources)
- [ ] FAQ + schema where useful
- [ ] descriptive internal links
- [ ] thin/duplicate pages removed or noindexed
